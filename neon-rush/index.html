About Neon Rush

Neon Rush is a high-octane 3D endless runner set on a glowing highway slicing through an electric cyberpunk metropolis. Built from the ground up using modern HTML5 Canvas, Vanilla JavaScript, and the Web Audio API, Neon Rush delivers authentic arcade gameplay with zero external heavy dependencies.

How to Play Neon Rush

You control an advanced cyber-runner moving at breakneck speed along a three-lane futuristic highway. Your objective is to survive as long as possible, achieve the highest distance score, and gather glowing cyber tokens and power-ups:

  • Dodge Road Hazards: Switch rapidly between the left, center, and right lanes to bypass impenetrable tall cyber walls.
  • Leap Over Hurdles: Jump over low laser barricades to clear them safely and collect airborne parabolic coin trails.
  • Duck Beneath High Beams: Slide into a low-profile hover dash to glide safely underneath high-voltage overhead plasma gates.
  • Collect Power-Ups: Grab energy shields to absorb fatal collisions, magnetic converters to draw nearby coins directly to you, and 2X score multipliers to maximize your run.

Controls Reference

Desktop Keyboard

  • A / D or ← / → : Change Lanes
  • W / ↑ or Space : Jump
  • S / ↓ : Slide & Fast-Fall Slam Dive
  • P or Esc : Pause / Resume

Mobile & Touch Gestures

  • Swipe Left / Right: Shift between lanes
  • Swipe Up: Jump over hurdles and grab jump coins
  • Swipe Down: Slide beneath overhead beams
  • Virtual D-Pad: On-screen directional buttons enabled automatically or toggled in Settings

Pro Tips for High Scores

  1. Master the Fast-Fall: If you are airborne from a jump and spot an overhead laser beam immediately ahead, swipe or press Down to execute a fast-fall dive directly into a slide.
  2. Follow Coin Jump Arcs: Parabolic coin formations are tailored to the exact trajectory of your jump. Jumping at the start of an arc guarantees you collect every coin in the curve.
  3. Listen to Audio Cues: Distinct procedural sound sweeps notify you of coin collection, shield activation, and obstacle proximity even when focusing your eyes on the horizon.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is Neon Rush free to play?
Yes, Neon Rush is 100% free to play directly in any modern desktop or mobile browser without downloads, installs, or paid upgrades.
How does Neon Rush save my high score?
Your best score, total collected coins, and audio preferences are securely saved locally to your device via browser LocalStorage.
Can I play offline?
Yes, all visuals, procedural audio, and game physics are self-contained with zero external CDN dependencies, allowing the game to run seamlessly without an active internet connection once loaded.
